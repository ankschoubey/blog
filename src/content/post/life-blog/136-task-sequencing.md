Here is how I sequence my personal and professional tasks in a way that it is most productive.

When I start, I look at the things i needed to do yesterday but they aren't complete. i.e Pending Tasks.

I will either schedule them or if they are small complete it right there.

Then I try to pick tasks that have wait time in them.

For example:

- Personal: If I am learning something, there is some wait time after which I can learn again.
- Work: If I unblock someone or run a CI pipeline, there will be some wait for me before I can work on it again.

This way, I have unblocked and scheduled things to run. Agents can take over.

Agents in this case is my unconsious, CI system, collegue or an AI agent. But mainly I am free.

i.e. I am taking things off my plate into someone elses plate.

I can work on something else in the mean time.

I will do this one task at a time in mono-task fashion. One task, enough completed and scheduled.

This does not include prioritization of tasks.

***

This is mostly how I like to sequence my tasks.
